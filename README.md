# Realistic-Tribute_EndlessSky

When a player goes through the process of dominating a faction, even if they win and tribute all the planets. They don't get the permission to buy outfits at the outfitter. That isn't realistic. One pesky outfitter shouldn't be able to hold up to the might of a species-conquering army. So, once you have defeated all of the planets in a faction, you get all their licenses! Very simple. Of course, getting the Navy Carrier license or something like that would be very difficult, but that's none of my business.

If a faction does not sell their tech at any outfitters, but you dominate them, the most technologically advanced planet in the species gets an outfitter with all of their tech on it. Same goes for ships.
